Summary

Here is how I would launch Wilden Grove on Meta. I would run your $100/day across three campaigns, test 6 creatives in the Netherlands and Belgium for 14 days, then move the winners into a scale campaign. The goal for Month 1 is to learn which ads, angles and market perform best. Results depend on your product, website, offer and market, so no number in this plan is a guarantee of sales or ROAS.

The math behind every decision below

  • Your brief gives an AOV of ~$100 and a break-even ROAS of 1.6x, which means break-even CPA = $62.50 ($100 ÷ 1.6).
  • At the break-even CPA, $100/day would produce about 1.6 purchases a day, or ~11 a week (a calculation, not a forecast). That is too little data to feed many ad sets, so I would use 3 campaigns and 5 ad sets, not 15.
  • Your monthly spend comes to $3,000 ($100 × 30 days). I also show a $2,000 version in section 4.

The first three moves

  1. Before launch (3 working days): I verify the Meta Pixel and Conversions API on your Shopify store, EU cookie consent, and your product catalog before any spend.
  2. Day 1: I launch 6 creatives (3 UGC videos, 2 statics, 1 carousel) with English copy into one test campaign, split by country so the Netherlands and Belgium are measured cleanly.
  3. Lead with trust. Your closest competitor, Vovia Bags, is rated 3.2–3.5 on Trustpilot with complaints about quality and returns, while you already offer free shipping, duties and taxes included, and 30-day risk-free returns.

I have assumed the budget and AOV in your brief are in USD. If your ad account bills in EUR, I will convert at the account rate.

3Campaign structure

Strategy 1: Always-on test and scale

Three campaigns, five ad sets and 16 ads go live on Day 1, which is a structure $100/day can feed.

C1 and C3 use ad set budgets, not campaign budget optimization, so the NL vs BE split stays fixed and comparable. Every campaign optimizes for Purchases. Both countries are targeted by location, with English-language ads. After Day 15, C1's $45/day splits two to one ($30 NL, $15 BE). C2 starts empty and receives only proven ads. C3 excludes recent buyers and stays at $15/day until the audience grows.

7Testing and optimization

We test one variable at a time, in order: angle first, then hook, then format and offer. Ads are judged by spend, not by days, because $100/day produces very few purchases.

Test order

  1. Days 1–14: which angle wins (trust, style, value, gifting, offer clarity).
  2. Days 15–21: which hook wins inside the best angle (new first 3 seconds, same body).
  3. Days 22–30: format and offer (UGC vs static, bundle vs single bag).

What counts as a winner: an ad with at least $125 spend (2× break-even CPA), at least 2 purchases and a CPA of $62.50 or lower. A strong winner is at $50 or lower.

Routine

  • Daily check of spend, purchases and tracking health; no edits inside 72 hours of an ad set starting.
  • Reviews on Days 7, 14, 21 and 28 with a written note: what we learned, what we kill, what we launch next.
  • If there are fewer than 8 purchases across the test campaign by Day 7, we check tracking first. If tracking is clean, we move the test ad sets to Add to Cart optimization to speed up learning, and return to Purchase in the scale campaign.

8Killing ads

No ad is paused for "feeling slow". Each ad gets a fixed spend limit tied to the $62.50 break-even CPA, which limits how much a weak ad can spend before we stop it.

LevelTriggerAction
Ad, early signal$20 spent and over 1,000 impressions, with link CTR under 0.6% or hook rate under 20%Pause. The creative is not earning attention.
Ad, interest signal$40 spent and 0 add to cartsPause.
Ad, purchase signal$62.50 spent (1× break-even CPA), 0 purchases and cost per add to cart over $20Pause. If cost per add to cart is under $14, allow it to $125.
Ad, hard stop$125 spent (2× break-even CPA) and CPA over $90Pause, whatever the other metrics say.
Ad set3 days, $150 spent and 0–1 purchasesPause the ad set and shift its budget to the stronger market.
Campaign$1,000 cumulative spend (about Day 10) and blended ROAS under 0.8xStop launching new ads and audit tracking, landing page and offer before spending more.

Guardrails against killing too early

  • No ad is judged in its first 24 hours or under 1,000 impressions.
  • If Meta gives an ad under $15 after 72 hours, that ad has not been tested. We move it into its own ad set for a fair read instead of calling it a loser.
  • A paused winner is never deleted. We archive its creative and hook so it can be recycled in Month 2.

9Scaling

We scale only what is clearly above break-even, in 20% steps, and we move proven ads into a separate campaign instead of raising the test budget.

Scale trigger: 3 days in a row at ROAS 1.9x or higher (CPA $52 or lower, 1.2× the break-even) and at least 5 purchases in that window.

MoveRule
Vertical (more budget)+20% at most every 72 hours. $100 becomes $120, $144, then $173 a day.
Move winners to C2On Day 15, the 4–6 best ads move into the Advantage+ scale campaign at $40/day, so testing keeps running untouched.
New anglesEach winning angle gets 2 new hooks (section 7) before any new angle is tried.
Roll backIf ROAS falls 25% below its pre-scale level for 3 days, undo the last budget increase.

Month 2 budget gate (Day 30): if blended ROAS is 1.6x or higher, we propose $150/day. If it is 1.2x–1.6x, we hold $100/day and keep testing. Below 1.2x, we stop and review the offer and landing page first.

Q4 timing: Black Friday is 27 November and Cyber Monday 30 November. Ad costs usually rise in the weeks around them, so we test early (this plan finishes around 20 November if launched this week), avoid big scaling jumps in the 10 days before, and I need to know now what offer you plan to run.

1130-day roadmap

Launch on Day 1, after three working days of setup. Each week ends with a review that decides what is killed, what is launched and where the budget moves.

Pre-launch (3 working days before Day 1)

  • Check the Meta Pixel and Conversions API through Shopify's Meta channel: test purchase fires, Event Match Quality is 6+ out of 10, and the domain is verified.
  • Confirm EU cookie consent is working and that GA4 purchase events and UTM parameters match Shopify orders.
  • Sync the Shopify catalog to Meta for the retargeting ad.
  • Confirm with you that EUR prices include VAT, plus shipping origin, delivery time and returns address for the Netherlands and Belgium.
  • Build all three campaigns in draft and run the Ad Library checklist from section 1.

Days 1–7: launch and listen

  • Publish C1 (NL and BE ad sets, 6 ads each) and C3 retargeting at $15/day.
  • Day 3: check delivery and ad approvals only, no edits.
  • Day 7 review: apply the early kill rules, note hook rate, CTR and cost per add to cart per ad, and brief the creative team on iteration 1.

Days 8–14: iterate

  • Launch iteration 1 (4 new ads).
  • Pause losers by the section 8 rules.
  • Day 14 review: compare NL and BE CPA, pick the 4–6 best ads, and confirm the budget shift to the scale campaign.

Days 15–21: scale what works

  • Launch C2 (Advantage+ sales campaign) at $40/day with the 4–6 winners. Drop C1 to $45/day.
  • Launch iteration 2 (3 ads) with new hooks on the winning angle.
  • Day 21 review: check the winner rule and the scale trigger.

Days 22–30: optimize and decide

  • Launch iteration 3 (3 ads): bundle or offer test, plus the best format.
  • Raise any qualifying ad set by 20% every 72 hours; hold everything else.
  • Agree the Black Friday offer and creative with you.
  • Day 30: written report with ROAS, CPA, winners, market comparison and a scale, hold or stop recommendation for Month 2.

Reporting: a one-page update every Monday (spend, purchases, CPA, ROAS, top 3 ads) and a live call at each Day 7, 14, 21 and 30 review.

12Final recommendations

My recommendation is to prioritize tracking, then trust-led UGC, then strict kill rules. The plan is built around your budget, and it makes no guarantee of sales or ROAS.

Priorities, in order

  1. Tracking and consent before spend. With about 11 purchases a week at break-even, every missed event slows learning.
  2. Trust-led UGC in the Netherlands first. It targets the gap left by Vovia's quality and returns complaints and uses the creative you already produce.
  3. Kill and scale by the written rules, so decisions come from the data and not from the daily mood of the dashboard.

What I need from you to sharpen the plan

  • A 90-day Shopify and GA4 export (orders, AOV, conversion rate) and access to any previous Meta ad account data. Your homepage carries a Meta domain verification tag, so there may be history.
  • Where your bags ship from, plus delivery time and returns address for the Netherlands and Belgium.
  • Confirmation that EUR prices display with VAT.
  • How many genuine customer reviews you can use in ads.
  • Your planned Black Friday offer and your creative team's weekly capacity.
  • For the drops: stock per denim style, your email or SMS list size, and delivery time for the gifting order cut-off.
